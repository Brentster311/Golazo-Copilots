# FRC-004 Domain Expert Notes

## Domain guidance
- Treat outputs as planning guidance, not tax filing advice.
- Expose transparent inputs (rate, days elapsed, YTD taxable income) used in projection.
- Keep threshold alerts focused on budget overrun and withholding gap only.
- Use deterministic ordering and stable severity classification.

## Risks
- Annualization can overreact early in the year with sparse income data.
- Users may infer legal precision from advisory outputs.
