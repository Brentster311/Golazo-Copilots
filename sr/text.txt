Throughout the correspondence, various troubleshooting steps were proposed and discussed. The support team suggested that a global admin from the second tenant must sign in to the application and accept the approval prompt on behalf of the organization. The team also recommended conducting a screen-sharing session to facilitate better understanding and resolution of the issue.

Despite these efforts, the case faced delays due to the unavailability of key personnel and the holiday season. The support team made multiple follow-up attempts to engage with the company, seeking confirmation on the admin roles of the users involved and their availability for meetings.
